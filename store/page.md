# Kinbound — itch.io page copy

## Short pitch
> The Wildmere keeps what you catch. Catch kin, feed them, put them to
> work — then push deeper for rarer species, crowned spires, and the
> gauntlet that guards the far shore.

## About
Kinbound is a creature-collecting survival sandbox. You land on the
Wildmere with nothing but a few capture orbs. Every kin you catch can
fight, haul, farm, ranch, or ride — your roster is your workforce,
your transport, and your army.

## The loop
- **Catch** — weaken wild kin and throw orbs. Level gates matter:
  overreach and the ball just breaks.
- **Build** — kinbox, beds, feed boxes, workbenches, farms, walls,
  towers. Assigned kin work the base while you explore.
- **Explore** — meadows to stone fields to the far shore; day/night
  spawns, weather that changes what bites, expeditions that run
  while you're away.
- **Crown the spires** — five element spires gate the endgame; raids
  escalate as you claim them; the gauntlet waits at the shore.
- **Condense** — feed spare kin to your favorites. Stars raise stats.

## Multiplayer (Windows/Android)
Direct-connect co-op: one player hosts, friends join by IP. The host
owns the world; guests keep private rosters under their keeper tag —
disconnect orphans your kin safely and rejoining reclaims them.

## Controls
- **Keyboard/mouse** — WASD, mouse aims, orbs, dodge, build/chat panels
- **Gamepad** — full navigation, RT throws down the facing line
- **Touch** — virtual stick (rim-push = sprint), verb buttons; on
  Android, panels summon the OS keyboard for chat and join-IP

## Honest limitations
- Save file carries the whole island: roster, structures, tech, time.
- Windows + Web builds ship today. Android exports a signed debug APK
  (sideload-ready); Play-signing awaits a release keystore.
- Audio is fully procedural (pads, arps, cries, UI) — no licensed tracks.
- 中文界面可用（设置里切换语言）。
